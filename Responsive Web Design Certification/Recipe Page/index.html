Lucky Pancakes

What will we ever do without luck and some charms? Here's how to do them effectively

Ingredients

  • 3 Eggs
  • 2 Tbsp Sugar
  • Olive Oil
  • 1 Tsp Honey (optional)

Instructions

  1. Whisk the Eggs until smooth
  2. Add 2 Tbsp of sugar and mix well until fully dissolved
  3. Slowly pour the mixture into a heated pan
  4. Don't let cats rule the world
